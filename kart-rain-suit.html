Kart Rain Suit

Weather-ready protection built to keep drivers dry, comfortable, and focused through changing track conditions and wet sessions.

Product Description

BUHASA SPORTS Kart Rain Suit is built for wet conditions, helping drivers stay dry and comfortable while preserving movement during changing track conditions. It is ideal for team use, club events, and OEM programs requiring reliable weather protection.

  • Water-resistant protection
  • Flexible movement for active driving
  • Comfort in wet conditions
  • Useful for travel, storage, and race-day preparation

Care & Maintenance

Clean with a soft cloth and mild soap. Dry naturally away from heat sources. Store in a cool, dry place when not in use to maintain flexibility and finish.
