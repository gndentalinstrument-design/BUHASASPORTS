Kart Rib Protector — Silver Guard

Lightweight, impact-focused rib protection designed for kart drivers and teams—comfortable, discreet, and built for repeated track use.

Product Description

The BUHASA Kart Rib Protector provides discreet, effective protection for drivers. Designed to absorb and diffuse impacts, it improves comfort during aggressive driving and repeated track sessions.

  • Impact-dissipating foam core
  • Ergonomic wrap-around fit for secure placement
  • Breathable outer fabric for extended use
  • Lightweight design that doesn't restrict movement
  • Compatible with team branding and OEM options

Care & Maintenance

Wipe clean with a damp cloth and mild soap. Hang to dry. Avoid machine washing and strong solvents to preserve the protective foam.
