Manufacturing

Precision manufacturing for kart racewears & boxing equipment

From prototype to full-scale production, BUHASA SPORTS delivers OEM and private-label manufacturing with strict quality controls, modern facilities, and export-ready logistics.

Capabilities

What we manufacture

Kart Racewears

We produce full kart race suits, jackets, rib protectors, gloves, and teamwear using flame-retardant and performance fabrics tested to motorsport standards. Options include custom branding, embroidery, and multi-panel colourways.

  • FIA-style & custom race suits
  • Racing jackets & teamwear
  • Racing gloves & footwear
  • Reinforced seams and ergonomic cut

Boxing Equipment

We manufacture boxing gloves, headguards, punching bags, focus pads, and protective apparel. Our production emphasises durable materials, high-grade foam padding, and reliable closures for safe training and competition use.

  • Training, sparring, and competition gloves
  • Headguards and protective padding
  • Punching bags, speed bags, and mitts
  • Custom branding and colourways

OEM & Private Label

We offer end-to-end OEM manufacturing: tech packs, sample development, prototyping, grading, production, and packaging. Minimum order quantities are flexible depending on product complexity — we work with startups and established brands.

Services: Short-run sampling, bulk production, private labelling, custom packaging, quality inspections, and full export documentation.

Materials & Components

We source premium textiles, high-density foams, reinforced threads, and hardware (zips, velcro, buckles) that meet durability and performance expectations for competitive sports.

  • Flame-retardant and breathable fabrics
  • Multi-density impact foams and liners
  • Durable seams, bar-tacking, and reinforcements
  • Eco-friendly options available on request
Process

Our manufacturing process

1 — Consultation & Specifications

We analyse your requirements, receive tech packs or sketches, and provide material and construction recommendations with transparent costing.

2 — Sampling & Prototyping

Rapid sample development with options for revisions. We test fit, materials, and performance before mass production.

3 — Production & Quality Control

Structured production lines with inline inspection and final QC checkpoints. We perform function and durability tests and record lot traceability.

4 — Packing & Export

Custom packaging, labelling, and export documentation. We coordinate logistics for sea, air, or courier shipments with consolidated export support.

FAQs

Common manufacturing questions

Minimum order quantity (MOQ)

MOQ varies by product. For many apparel items, MOQs start at 100–200 units but smaller pilot runs are often possible for sampling.

Can you private-label my brand?

Yes — we provide private-label solutions including labels, packaging, and custom trims. Tell us your requirements and we will include these in the quote.

What payment terms do you accept?

Typical terms: deposit on order (30–50%) and balance before shipment. We accept bank transfers (TT) and can discuss escrow or third-party payment for new clients.

How do you ensure quality?

Quality is managed through incoming material checks, inline audits, and final inspection. We welcome third-party QC or buyer inspections for critical orders.
