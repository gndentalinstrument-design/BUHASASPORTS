Kart Racing Shoes — Traction Pro

Lightweight kart racing shoes engineered for precise pedal feel, excellent traction, and durable performance. Available for teams, distributors and OEM orders.

Product Description

BUHASA Traction Pro kart racing shoes combine a low-profile sole for superb pedal feedback with durable uppers and secure closure systems. Designed for drivers who need consistent feel and control across long sessions.

  • Low-profile, grippy sole for pedal control
  • Secure closure and heel support for stability
  • Lightweight breathable uppers to reduce fatigue
  • Durable construction for frequent track use
  • Available in team colourways and OEM branding

Care & Maintenance

Wipe with a damp cloth after use. Use mild soap for stubborn dirt and allow to air dry. Avoid machine washing to preserve sole integrity and adhesives.
