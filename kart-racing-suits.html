Kart Cordura Race Suit — Pro Series

High‑performance Cordura kart racing suit engineered for durability, abrasion resistance and breathability. Available with full OEM customization: sublimation printing, embroidery, patches and private‑label finishing.

Product Description

Our Pro Series Kart Cordura Race Suit uses high-tenacity Cordura panels in wear-prone areas combined with breathable laminated fabric across the torso and arms. The result is a suit that withstands abrasion, provides tear resistance, and keeps drivers comfortable during extended track sessions.

  • Fabric: Cordura reinforced panels + breathable race fabric
  • Construction: Triple-stitched seams, bartacking at stress points
  • Protection: Reinforced knees, seat and elbow areas
  • Comfort: Stretch panels for articulation and integrated mesh for ventilation
  • Compliance: Designed for professional track use (check with your race organizer for local homologation requirements)

Care & Maintenance

Machine wash gentle cycle at low temperature. Do not tumble dry. Avoid bleach and harsh solvents. For sublimated or embroidered suits, follow any additional care instructions supplied with the product.
