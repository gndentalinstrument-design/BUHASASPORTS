Kart Balaclava

Comfort-focused, breathable head protection designed to improve fit, reduce sweat, and keep drivers fully focused through long, demanding sessions.

Product Description

The BUHASA SPORTS Kart Balaclava is designed for comfort, breathability, and secure coverage during intensive track activity. It sits close to the skin, helps reduce sweat buildup, and supports a more comfortable experience under the helmet.

  • Breathable comfort for long sessions
  • Secure, low-profile fit under helmet
  • Lightweight design for easy movement
  • Suitable for club drivers, teams, and OEM programs

Care & Maintenance

Hand wash gently with mild soap and cool water. Air dry naturally away from direct heat. Avoid bleach or harsh detergents to preserve the fabric feel and fit.
